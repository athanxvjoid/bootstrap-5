Every part earns its place.

Wavelength One has no components chosen to hit a price point. Each part was chosen, tested, and sometimes machined in-house because it measurably changed what you hear.

PLINTH

A quiet base, on purpose.

The plinth is a cast-aluminum shell wrapped in a walnut or ash veneer, floated on three elastomer-damped feet. Its density and internal bracing are tuned to push resonant frequencies well below the audio band, so footsteps and shelf vibration stay out of the groove.

PLATTER & DRIVE

Weight where it helps, decoupled where it matters.

A 1.6 kg machined-aluminum platter stores enough rotational energy to shrug off small speed disturbances. It's turned by a low-torque DC motor through an elastic belt, which mechanically isolates platter from motor noise — the reason belt-drive decks read quieter on a phono stage.

TONEARM & CARTRIDGE

Light, rigid, and set correctly from the factory.

The carbon-fiber tonearm rides on gimbal bearings for near-zero friction in every direction. Tracking force, anti-skate, and cartridge alignment are set and verified before the deck leaves the workshop — you just drop the needle.
